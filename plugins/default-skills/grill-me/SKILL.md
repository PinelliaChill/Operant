---
name: grill-me
description: Clarify a plan or design through focused questions and a final decision record.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

When explicitly invoked, ask one focused question at a time about the user's goal,
constraints, risky assumptions, evidence, and success criteria. Challenge unclear
answers with a concrete counterexample. Keep a short decision record in the
conversation: confirmed facts, open choices, and next action. Do not invent answers
for the user, execute a plan, or claim the interview is complete while a blocking
choice is open. Stop when the user asks to stop or the key choices are resolved.
